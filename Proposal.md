# SquareUp Proposal

## Part 1 questions

**What app are you cloning?**
I am cloning **Splitwise** as a native ios mobile app called **SquareUp**. It is an app which I personally use a lot and have seen every other college student use it. Splitwise lets people create groups, record shared expenses, divide costs, track balances, and record settlements. I chose it because its group structure works well for the many situations in which people share expenses, including meals, trips, roommates, and events.
I will be also adding the feature to create clubs or organizations, which will have a different structure allowing reimburesement tracking and differnt roles.

**What problem does this app solve (not features)?**
It solves the problem of coordinating shared spending clearly, usually groups struggle to know who paid, what each person owes, and what is still unsettled, and often leads to people hesitating to pay, due to the hassle of managing and asking everybody for settlement. Clubs and small organizations also struggle to track budgets, approvals, and reimbursements.


**What are you going to keep, change, or remove?**
I will keep Splitwise's core sense of  groups, members, recording who paid, receipts, adjustable splits, expense history, balances, settlements, and debt simplification. I will change it by adding clearer navigation and role identification, organization budgets, and an AI-assisted Live Receipt Room, with no restrictive free-version limits. 

I personally also feel that the app is not appealing and easy to the user, the User interafce and user expiernece has a lot of room to be worked upon. I myself do not feel like opening the app. lol 

I will remove bank transfers, complicated accounting integrations, and unrelated enterprise features from the version.
